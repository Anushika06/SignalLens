# Razorpay — intelligence brief
**For:** Cashfree Payments, a payment gateway for Indian businesses · **Focus:** Everything · **Date:** 1 Oct 2026 · **Official site:** [razorpay.com](https://razorpay.com)

Razorpay is a financial technology platform that helps businesses accept digital payments, manage transactions, process payouts, and access business finance tools. It offers payment gateway solutions, payment links, invoices, business banking, and payroll services for Indian businesses, with expanding services in the US market.

> **Executive summary.** Razorpay is a leading Indian fintech platform serving over 40,000 startups and businesses, recently expanding its AI-driven product suite with the launch of the 'Vulcan' AI payments foundation model and advancing toward a potential $600 million IPO. The company continues to innovate in AI-native payments, agentic banking, and biometric security while maintaining tailored pricing for startups and enterprises. For Cashfree Payments, this signals heightened competitive pressure in AI-powered payment infrastructure and market credibility, particularly as Razorpay moves closer to public market scrutiny.
